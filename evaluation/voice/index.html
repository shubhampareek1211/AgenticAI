Record speech evaluation samples

Start with the first two English questions. These recordings stay in your browser until you download them. This page does not upload audio.

Downloaded clips will later be sent through our application to Google Cloud Whisper for testing. Use only your voice or a speaker who has agreed. Avoid personal details.

Ready. Microphone access starts only when you press Record.

Put the audio files and manifest.json in the same folder under the project's .local/voice/recordings/ directory. Each download is a separate file. The manifest lists the recordings you chose to download; their contents stay on this device until the evaluation is run.

The complete set has 20 short questions, five long recordings and five no-speech controls per language group. A few recordings are useful for diagnosis but do not establish release acceptance.
